# Intent: document-eval-cadence

## Problem
The eval suite has no stated review cadence. Cases accumulate, and nobody knows
when a passing case has stopped being worth its runtime.

## Proposed outcome
The harness states when evals are reviewed and what justifies deleting a case.

## Affected users and systems
Anyone maintaining the harness. No runtime impact.

## Constraints
Must not add a scheduled job or a new dependency.

## Open questions
None material.

## Areas of concern
None — this contradicts no existing ADR or spec.
